After a successful first implementation in 2026, we are looking to repeat the experiment in 2027. We plan to use this event as a way to introduce Nextflow and nf-core to the local bioinformatics community in Okinawa so feel free to join even if you don't have any experience with Nextflow. People from outside of OIST are welcome, let us know if you'd like to join. We can host up to 20-25 people.
