Louis Le Nezet ([@LouisLeNezet](https://github.com/LouisLeNezet)) will present [nf-core/nft-utils](https://github.com/nf-core/nft-utils) v1.1.1, an nf-test plugin for capture-level testing. The update expands `sanitizeOutput()` with options for unstable keys, pattern matching, and MD5 replacements via nft-bam and nft-vcf.
