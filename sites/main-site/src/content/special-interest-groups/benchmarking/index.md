# Introduction

The Benchmarking Special Interest Group provides a centralized home within nf-core for performance and accuracy testing across all bioinformatics workflows. While initiated by the development of the nf-core/variantbenchmarking pipeline, this group expands the scope to include any type of method comparison, validation, or resource efficiency study.

### Core Objectives

- Software Development: Modularizing benchmarking components in nf-core so other developers can easily plug-and-play validation steps into their pipelines.
- Community Needs Forum: Identifying what researchers need to compare (ranging from metagenomics and spatial transcriptomics to drug response prediction) and prioritizing tool development accordingly.
- Network Syntonization: Keeping nf-core aligned with external benchmarking standards and platforms like NCBench, openEBench, and Open Problems in Single-Cell Analysis.

# Activities

Our format revolves around our community blog and a [monthly meeting](/special-interest-groups/benchmarking/meetings.md). Each meeting features a 30 minute presentation followed by 30 minutes of round table discussions and feedback on selected topics. Presentations will cover a diverse range of areas, including:

- Benchmarking projects detailing both methods and results
- New software tools in the field
- Infrastructure benchmarking
- Inspiring talks from leading genomics researchers
- Ongoing novel efforts in benchmarking
- Technical sessions providing practical examples of applying nf-core pipelines to benchmarking
