[MCBLab/NetPerturb](https://github.com/MCBLab/NetPerturb) is a scalable Nextflow pipeline designed to infer Gene Regulatory Networks (GRNs) and calculate single-cell expression ranking perturbation scores using the scRank algorithm.

Este projeto de hackathon tem como foco continuar o desenvolvimento desse pipeline.
